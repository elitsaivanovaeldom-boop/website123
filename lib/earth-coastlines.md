# Coastline source

Simplified outer land outlines from Natural Earth, 1:110m, public domain.
Small islands below two square degrees were omitted for legibility.
Coordinates use longitude/latitude and are rounded to two decimals.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The outline is simplified to keep the small globe legible.
